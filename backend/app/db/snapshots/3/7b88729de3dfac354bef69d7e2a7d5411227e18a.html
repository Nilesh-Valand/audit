Classic ASP Modernization and Migration

Classic ASP apps are incredibly resilient. Many are still quietly running core business logic decades after they were written. But keeping them alive gets painful when you need to integrate with modern APIs, recruit developers who actually know VBScript, or move away from fragile on-prem servers. Upgrading your system fixes that technical debt before it becomes an emergency.

Auditing What You Have

We don't guess. We audit your codebase, infrastructure, third-party COM components, and database integrations to see exactly what you're dealing with, then build a realistic roadmap for the transition.

Upgrading in Stages

You don't need a risky, ground-up rewrite that takes your system offline. We update individual modules, decouple spaghetti code, and swap out outdated components piece by piece. Your core application stays up and running while we move things over.

Business Logic Preservation

Years of business knowledge are often embedded within Classic ASP systems. We preserve critical workflows and functionality while modernizing the underlying technology.

Database & Infrastructure Upgrades

We improve database performance, strengthen security, and modernize supporting infrastructure without disrupting operations.

Classic ASP to .NET Migration

For organizations ready to move forward, we help migrate Classic ASP to ASP.NET Core and modern .NET platforms. Our phased migration approach reduces risk while allowing continuous business operations.

Testing & Validation

Every migration stage includes comprehensive testing to ensure functionality, performance, and data integrity remain intact.Whether your goal is partial modernization or a complete Classic ASP to .NET migration, we provide a structured path forward.
