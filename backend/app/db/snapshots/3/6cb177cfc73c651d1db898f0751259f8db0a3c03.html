ASP.NET WebForms Modernization - Migrate to ASP.NET Core or .NET 10

ASP.NET WebForms apps are becoming harder to maintain and modernize. And they are falling way behind modern cloud alternatives on performance. Our modernization services help you upgrade at your own pace without breaking the core business logic your teams rely on.

Assessment & Roadmap

We do an audit of your codebase, map the dependencies, spot the risky spots, then lay out a phased migration plan before we touch a single line.

Incremental Migration

With Microsoft's YARP reverse proxy and side-by-side hosting strategies, we move over page by page. So, risk stays low and parallel testing is actually possible.

Business Logic Preservation

The core workflows, validation rules and integrations get carried forward as is. We refactor the UI layers, but we do not rebuild what already works.

Database & ORM Modernisation

When it makes sense, we migrate from raw ADO.NET or DataSets toward Entity Framework Core. Helping maintainability and also tends to improve query performance a bit.

Full WebForms to .NET 10 Migration

We rebuild the app on modern ASP.NET Core, Razor Pages, or Blazor. The original WebForms application stays in place as the functional specification.

Testing & Validation

At each stage we run comprehensive regression testing. By continuously verifying performance, we deliver a rock-solid product that maintains a consistent user experience.

WebForms Bug Fixing, Performance Tuning, and IIS Support

The One Technologies team specialises in diagnosing and resolving exactly these kinds of challenges.

ViewState Optimization

Huge ViewState is usually why old WebForms pages crawl. We look under the hood at your page hierarchy to find out which controls are hoarding data. Shut it off where it's not doing anything, or move it to Session state instead. You can usually strip 40% to 70% off the total page weight this way. And your forms will still work exactly the same.

WebForms Performance Tuning

We track down performance lag wherever it lives. Whether that's a hanging database query, heavy application logic, or slow rendering. To pinpoint the exact issue, we profile the stack using SQL Profiler, Application Insights with ANTS Performance Profiler. Once we see what's causing the slowdown, we fix it with practical steps like query optimization, output caching, HTTP compression and offloading static assets.

WebForms Bug Fixing

If you've ever spent an afternoon tracing a ghost bug through the WebForms page lifecycle, you know exactly how brutal it is. It’s the kind of debugging that makes most developers want to log off for the day. Our team sticks around to solve them. We dig into the framework to find out exactly why the state broke. Fix it and write down the root cause so no one else has to lose time to the same bug.

IIS Support & Configuration

If your IIS server is throwing 503 errors, recycling at the worst possible moments, or dropping connections, we can fix it. We handle the technical side of IIS that usually causes headaches. Such as tuning application pools, setting up predictable worker process recycling policies, and writing URL rewrite rules that don't break your routing.

Session & Authentication Issues

We fix the broken session states and authentication cookies that trigger errors for your users in production. We find the root cause and get it running right. By reinforcing your security protocols and stabilizing session persistence, we ensure your users stay securely logged in without frustrating interruptions.

Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can I onboard a dedicated ASP.NET WebForms developer from The One Technologies

We can get a dedicated ASP.NET WebForms developer on your project in 3 to 5 days. We talk through what you need. Just hop on a quick call to shortlist candidates, and sort out the engagement model. If it’s a standard build, we can usually wrap up the whole onboarding process in under a week.

What is the difference between hiring a dedicated ASP.NET WebForms developer and hiring on an hourly basis?

If you hire a dedicated ASP.NET WebForms developer, they’re all yours for the length of the contract. They stick to your project, get to know your codebase inside out, and you don't have to compete for their time. Hourly hiring is better if you just need a hand with small, random tasks. Think quick bug fixes, adding a single feature, or doing a code review for a few hours a month.

Do you provide ongoing legacy ASP.NET support for applications built on older versions of the .NET Framework?

If you have older WebForms apps running on .NET Framework 2.0 through 4.8? You don't necessarily need to rewrite them from scratch. We keep legacy ASP.NET applications running smoothly on Windows Server and IIS by handling the chores. Security patches, database maintenance, and broken dependencies. You get to keep your stable, secure system online without forcing an immediate, painful migration on your team.

What does your WebForms application support SLA look like?

We tier our support SLAs by severity to ensure you get help when you need it most. If you hit a critical production issue? Like the application going down or a risk of data loss. We will work on it continuously until it is fixed. For high priority bugs that disrupt core functionality, our team will dive in within four business hours. Standard enhancements and non-critical issues go right into our normal schedule. Usually falling into the next agreed sprint or maintenance window. You can find the exact SLA terms spelled out in your support contract.

How long does a typical WebForms to .NET 10 migration take?

How long a migration takes really comes down to three things. The size of your app, the state of your current code, and how many third party systems it talks to. If you are moving a small to medium WebForms app? Say 20 to 50 pages and a single database? You are usually looking at 8 to 16 weeks for a full rewrite to ASP.NET Core. Or .NET 10 For massive enterprise systems packed with complex business logic. A total flip overnight isn't realistic. Those usually require a phased approach spread out over 6 to 12 months. We can give you a firm, accurate timeline once we actually get under the hood and look at your codebase.

Do you have to rewrite the entire application to migrate WebForms to ASP.NET Core?

If you're worried about downtime or risking a massive break, a slow migration is usually the smarter play. You can actually run the new ASP.NET Core app side-by-side with your old WebForms setup. From there, you just peel off pages or modules piece by piece. It keeps you from going completely offline, makes troubleshooting way easier, and lets your team test things out in the wild without committing to a terrifying "go-live" day.

What tools do you use for WebForms performance tuning and diagnosing ViewState issues?

We split our profiling between the backend and the browser. For the application layer, we rely on tools like SQL Profiler, Application Insights, dotTrace, or ANTS. On the frontend, we just use Chrome DevTools to track page weight and loading bottlenecks. ViewState is usually the biggest culprit for bloated pages. We dig into the page trace and control trees to find the exact controls that are hoarding state unnecessarily, and then we disable it where it isn't needed.

How do you protect intellectual property when we outsource ASP.NET WebForms development to your team?

We start every outsourcing project by signing an NDA. From there, your code stays in private, access-controlled repositories, and only the developers assigned to your project can touch it. For highly sensitive projects, we enforce a strict clean-desk policy in our workspace. When the project wraps up, we return your assets and completely purge your code from our systems. If you already have a preferred security framework, just let us know. We're happy to work within your rules.
