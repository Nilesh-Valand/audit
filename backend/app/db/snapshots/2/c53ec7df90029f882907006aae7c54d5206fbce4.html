Frequently Asked Questions

Real questions from real users before going solar — answered clearly to help you decide with confidence.

general

What is SolarSathi?

SolarSathi is an AI-powered solar marketplace that helps homeowners, businesses, and industries compare solar solutions, estimate savings, check subsidies, discover verified installers, and make informed decisions about going solar.

general

Is SolarSathi a solar installation company?

No. SolarSathi is an independent platform that connects users with verified solar vendors and provides tools like solar calculators, subsidy guidance, and quote comparison to help users choose the best solution.

general

Is using SolarSathi free?

Yes. You can use our solar calculator, subsidy checker, AI assistant, and vendor discovery tools completely free.

general

Which locations does SolarSathi serve?

SolarSathi supports customers across India. Service availability depends on your city and the presence of verified installation partners.

general

How does SolarSathi help me choose the right solar system?

We analyze your electricity usage, location, roof type, and requirements to recommend an appropriate system size, estimated savings, and nearby verified vendors.

Solar Calculator

How does the Solar Calculator work?

Simply enter your monthly electricity bill or electricity consumption. Our calculator estimates the recommended system size, annual generation, investment, savings, subsidy eligibility, and payback period.

Solar Calculator

Are the calculator results accurate?

The results are estimates based on your inputs and standard assumptions. Actual savings may vary depending on your location, roof conditions, electricity tariff, weather, and installation quality.

Can I calculate savings without knowing my electricity units?

Yes. You can estimate your solar requirements using either your monthly electricity bill amount or electricity consumption.

Does the calculator include government subsidies?

Yes. Eligible residential users may see estimated subsidy benefits based on applicable government schemes. Actual eligibility depends on current regulations.

Can businesses use the calculator?

Yes. Commercial and industrial users can also estimate system size, savings, and return on investment.
