Andaman & Nicobar Islands Solar Energy Policy 2021 / A&N Rooftop Solar (ANIDCO / A&N Electricity Dept.)

Andaman & Nicobar Islands · Andaman & Nicobar Islands Integrated Development Corporation (ANIDCO) / A&N Electricity Dept.

For residential, commercial, eco-tourism/hotel, institutional, off-grid island in Andaman & Nicobar Islands — this guide covers the Andaman & Nicobar Islands Solar Energy Policy 2021 / A&N Rooftop Solar (ANIDCO / A&N Electricity Dept.). Administered by Andaman & Nicobar Islands Integrated Development Corporation (ANIDCO) / A&N Electricity Dept.. Below are subsidy rates, eligibility, technical rules, documents, and scheme process.

Subsidy benefits

  • Central CFA (PM Surya Ghar): up to Rs.78,000
  • additional 10% island UT special category
  • A&N UT additional: 30% UT subsidy
  • Off-grid island solar (remote uninhabited-turned-inhabited islands): 95% subsidy
  • Eco-tourism solar: additional 20% tourism incentive under ANIDCO

Authority and scope

  • Government body: Andaman & Nicobar Islands Integrated Development Corporation (ANIDCO) / A&N Electricity Dept.
  • Nodal agency: A&N Islands Electricity Department / ANIDCO
  • Eligible for: residential, commercial, eco-tourism/hotel, institutional, off-grid island
  • Installation type: Rooftop on-grid (Port Blair) + off-grid solar + BESS (remote islands)

Eligibility and disbursement

  • Open to multiple consumer categories.
  • Income restrictions: No income restriction; indigenous Andamanese and Nicobarese tribal communities get 95% off-grid subsidy.
  • Disbursement timeline: 60–120 days post-commissioning (shipping/logistics to islands)
  • Disbursement mode: DBT via A&N Electricity Dept./ANIDCO; UT subsidy via A&N Administration
  • Maximum capacity: 500 kW

Net metering and technical rules

  • Net metering is available under this scheme.
  • Net metering policy: A&N IERC Net Metering Regulations
  • 1 kW–500 kW. Single DISCOM: A&N Electricity Dept. (Port Blair grid) + individual island microgrids.
  • Gross metering policy: A&N IERC gross metering for >500 kW (very limited — island size constraints).
  • Hybrid policy: A&N Island Solar + BESS: every island project mandates battery storage (intermittent grid). Offshore wind study ongoing for Car Nicobar.
  • DISCOM requirements: A&N Electricity Dept.: Port Blair grid. Remote island microgrids: ANIDCO/SECI managed individual island solar power plants.

How to apply

Apply on A&N Administration portal or pmsuryaghar.gov.in

A&N Electricity Dept. feasibility check

ANIDCO-empanelled vendor selection (must have island logistics capability)

Installation + net meter (or BESS for remote islands)

Electricity Dept. inspection + commissioning

Enhanced central CFA + UT subsidy via DBT

Documents required

  • Aadhaar card
  • Electricity bill (A&N consumer number)
  • Property documents (A&N land grant / lease)
  • Bank account details
  • Tribal/indigenous certificate (for priority)

Additional details

  • Scheme budget: A&N RE target: 100% renewable energy by
  • PM Surya Ghar special category (+10%) + island territory additional funding.
  • Target beneficiaries: All A&N consumers. Key areas: Port Blair (744xxx), Havelock Island (744xxx), Neil Island (744xxx). 572 islands — only ~38 inhabited. Remote islands: off-grid solar + BESS critical.
  • Progress: A&N: 30+ MW solar installed (April 2025). Neil Island: India's first island to achieve 100% solar power (1.1 MW solar + BESS, 2018). Havelock Island: 80% solar powered. Port Blair: 30% solar in grid mix.
