Government schemes

Find subsidy schemes that apply to your state and installation type before choosing a nearby vendor.

  • Union Territory Government Policy

    Andaman & Nicobar Islands Solar Energy Policy 2021 / A&N Rooftop Solar (ANIDCO / A&N Electricity Dept.)

    Andaman & Nicobar Islands

    Central CFA (PM Surya Ghar): up to Rs.78,000 + additional 10% island UT special category. A&N UT additional: 30% UT subsidy. Off-grid island solar (remote uninhabited-turned-inhabited islands): 95% subsidy. Eco-tourism solar: additional 20% tourism incentive under ANIDCO.

    Andaman & Nicobar Islands Integrated Development Corporation (ANIDCO) / A&N Electricity Dept.

  • State Government Policy

    Andhra Pradesh Solar Power Policy 2022 / AP Rooftop Solar Programme (NREDCAP)

    Andhra Pradesh

    Central CFA: up to ₹78,000 (PM Surya Ghar). AP state additional: 25–30% residential top-up (NREDCAP-administered). AP Solar Policy 2022: wheeling charges waived for 5 years; banking charges waived. Agricultural solar pumps (PM KUSUM): 60% subsidy (30+30). AP Farmer Income Protection Scheme: free electricity to farmers (4,000 units/year).

    NREDCAP (New & Renewable Energy Development Corporation of AP) / Energy Dept., Govt. of AP

  • State Government Policy

    Andhra Pradesh Solar Power Policy 2022 / AP Rooftop Solar Programme (NREDCAP)

    Andhra Pradesh

    Central CFA: up to Rs.78,000 (PM Surya Ghar). AP state additional: 25–30% residential top-up (NREDCAP). AP Solar Policy 2022: wheeling charges waived for 5 years. Agricultural solar pumps: 60% subsidy.

    NREDCAP (New & Renewable Energy Development Corporation of AP) / Energy Dept., Govt. of AP

  • State Government Policy

    Arunachal Pradesh Solar Energy Policy 2018 (Updated 2022) / AP Rooftop Solar (APEDA / DoE)

    Arunachal Pradesh

    Central CFA (PM Surya Ghar): up to Rs.78,000 + additional 10% NE special category + additional MHA border area funding. APEDA state additional: 25% state subsidy. Off-grid border/tribal solar: 95% subsidy (highest in India). Agricultural solar pumps: 80% total (NE enhanced).

    Arunachal Pradesh Energy Development Agency (APEDA) / Arunachal Pradesh DoE (Dept. of Energy)

  • State Government Policy

    Assam Solar Energy Policy 2021 / Assam Rooftop Solar Programme (AEDA)

    Assam

    Central CFA (PM Surya Ghar): up to Rs.78,000 + additional 10% NE special category. Assam state additional: 20% state subsidy (AEDA). Agricultural solar pumps (PM KUSUM): 50% central (NE rate) + 30% state = 80% total for Assam farmers. Off-grid tea garden solar: 90% subsidy.

    Assam Energy Development Agency (AEDA) / APDCL (Assam Power Distribution Co. Ltd.)

  • State Government Policy

    Bihar Solar Energy Policy 2017 / Bihar Rooftop Solar Programme (BREDA)

    Bihar

    Central CFA (PM Surya Ghar): up to Rs.78,000. Bihar state additional: 30% state subsidy for residential below poverty line (BPL) consumers. Agricultural solar pumps (PM KUSUM): 60% total. Bihar Solar Policy: Rs.1,000/kW/year generation incentive for 5 years (residential net metering).

    Bihar Renewable Energy Development Agency (BREDA) / Bihar State Power Holding Co. Ltd (BSPHCL)

  • Central Government Scheme

    Central Public Sector Undertaking (CPSU) Scheme Phase II

    All States and UTs — Pan India

    VGF: ₹7.15 crore/MW (for DCR projects). Central govt / PSUs pay discounted tariff to developer; no direct outflow for building owner.

    MNRE / SECI; for Central Govt. offices, PSUs, defence, railways

  • Union Territory Government Policy

    Chandigarh Solar Energy Policy 2022 / Chandigarh Rooftop Solar Programme

    Chandigarh

    Central CFA (PM Surya Ghar): up to Rs.78,000. Chandigarh UT: 30% additional UT subsidy for residential. Net metering benefit: Chandigarh has highest net metering buy-back rate in North India (Rs.3.05/unit). Solar mandatory for all new buildings >500 sqm.

    Department of Science & Technology, UT of Chandigarh / CPDCL (Chandigarh Power Distribution Company Ltd.)

  • State Government Policy

    Chhattisgarh Solar Energy Policy 2017-27 / CG Rooftop Solar Programme (CREDA)

    Chhattisgarh

    Central CFA (PM Surya Ghar): up to Rs.78,000. CG state additional: 30% state subsidy (CREDA). Agricultural solar pumps (PM KUSUM): 60% total. Tribal off-grid solar (Naxal-affected Bastar): 90% subsidy. Surya Rajya Yojana: free solar pumps for 1 lakh small/marginal farmers.

    Chhattisgarh State Renewable Energy Development Agency (CREDA) / CSPDCL
